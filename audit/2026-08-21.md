## Pillar 1: Content Quality, Completeness & Persona Alignment

### 1.1 Content Quality & Methodological Rigor

#### A. Data Enablement (`docs/data_enablement/index.md`)
* **Core Purpose**: Explains how hospital clinical data is dimensioned, deployed, and prepared for federated or centralized research.
* **Strengths**:
  * Clear 3-stage lifecycle: **Dimensioning** (Data Landscape & Feasibility Report), **Deployment** (ELT, Automated Terminology Mapping [ATM], Natural Language Processing [NLP], Anonymization), and **Readiness** (Governance, Hospital Approval, Master Services Agreements).
  * Highly effective analogies translating complex ML/NLP concepts into clinical trial terms (e.g., comparing ATM to WHODrug/MedDRA dictionary coding, and NLP to a team of CRAs abstracting eCRFs).
  * Clear visual diagrams for ATM, NLP, Anonymization, and Enablement.
* **Gaps & Improvement Areas**:
  * Missing quantitative benchmarks for NLP/ATM turnaround times or typical vocabulary coverage rates beyond example snippets.
  * Lacks explicit guidance on how data quality feedback loops from downstream analysis feed back into the enablement pipeline.

#### B. Data Mediation (`docs/data_mediation/`)
* **Core Purpose**: Defines the 5-stage governed workflow connecting Data Holders (hospitals) with Data Users (researchers).
* **Page Breakdown**:
  * `index.md`: Thoroughly articulates the 5 stages: *Mediation Request*, *Hospital-Led Approval*, *Data Validation and QA*, *Compliance & Delivery*, and *Immutable Audit Trail*.
  * `data_solutions.md`: Details the four core data delivery products: **Patient Count (PC)** (feasibility query), **Aggregated Patient Characterisation (APC)** (federated analysis via Atlas or R), **Patient-Level Data (PLD)** (anonymized DuckDB OMOP dataset, analogous to SDTM), and **Patient Finder (PF)** (trial recruitment list kept hospital-side). Includes a clear product lifecycle mapping table.
  * `quality.md`: Deep methodology on Physician-led Verification (Instance-level and Patient-level rSDV, False Negative detection using semantic similarity, Fleiss’ Kappa = 0.81, Golden Annotators) combined with Kahn Framework-driven Validation (Conformance, Completeness, Plausibility via OHDSI Data Quality Dashboard [DQD]).
* **Strengths**:
  * Outstanding clinical credibility with formal references (Kahn et al., 2016; Quijada et al., 2022).
  * Clear delineation of data privacy boundaries (e.g., Patient Finder lists never leaving the hospital).
* **Gaps & Improvement Areas**:
  * `nav_order` gap in `docs/data_mediation/`: `data_solutions.md` is `nav_order: 1`, `quality.md` is `nav_order: 3`, leaving `nav_order: 2` unassigned.

#### C. Data Analysis Core Guides (`docs/data_analysis/`)
* **`index.md` & `intro_to_observational_research.md`**:
  * Provides an exceptional conceptual bridge from randomized clinical trials (RCTs) and CDISC SDTM standards to observational RWD and OMOP CDM.
  * Explains the universal translator role of `concept_id`, clinical domains (`PERSON`, `OBSERVATION_PERIOD`, `CONDITION_OCCURRENCE`, `DRUG_EXPOSURE`, `MEASUREMENT`, `PROCEDURE_OCCURRENCE`), concept sets, cohort definitions, Table 1 baseline characterisation, and attrition tracking.
* **`performing_analysis.md`**:
  * Structures study execution into 6 concrete phases: *Study Setup & Configuration*, *Cohort Definition & Instantiation*, *Cohort Attrition Tracking*, *Phenotype & Cohort Validation*, *Analysis Pipeline*, and *Results Generation*.
  * Integrates modern tooling including `OmopStudyBuilder`, `CDMConnector`, `CohortConstructor`, `PhenotypeR`, `CohortCharacteristics`, and `visOmopResults`.
* **`setup.md` & `CodeToRun.R`**:
  * Clear onboarding guide for local R/RStudio/RTools setup, package installation, and Eunomia mock database configuration.
  * `CodeToRun.R` is self-contained, reproducible, and provides clear console assertions.

#### D. Standardised Study Guides (`docs/data_analysis/standard_studies/`)
The documentation provides 9 in-depth study design guides + 1 hub index:
1. **Population-Level Epidemiology**: Incidence rates, point/period prevalence, denominator cohort generation, time-at-risk.
2. **Patient-Level Characterisation**: Baseline clinical profiling, Table 1 generation, comorbidity scores, prior observation windows.
3. **Drug Utilisation (DUS)**: New-user design, treatment duration, cumulative dose, indications, persistence and adherence (Proportion of Patients Covered - PPC).
4. **Comparative Cohort Studies**: Causal inference, active comparator new-user designs, immortal time bias prevention, Kaplan-Meier survival curves, hazard ratios.
5. **Self-Controlled Designs**: Self-Controlled Case Series (SCCS) and Case-Risk Interval (SCRI), transient exposures, acute event timing, within-person confounding control.
6. **Patient-Level Prediction (PLP)**: Prognostic modeling, Target ($T$) vs Outcome ($O$) cohorts, time-at-risk, feature extraction, train/test splitting, ROC AUC, calibration.
7. **Pathway Analysis**: Treatment trajectories, lines of therapy (L1, L2, L3+), Sankey/Sunburst visual representations.
8. **Impact Evaluation Studies**: Quasi-experimental designs, Interrupted Time Series (ITS), Difference-in-Differences (DiD), regulatory Risk Minimisation Measures (RMM).
9. **Health Economics & HCRU (HEOR / HTA)**: Healthcare resource utilisation across inpatient/ICU/outpatient, direct medical costing, Markov state-transition models, probabilistic sensitivity analysis (PSA), ICER, Net Monetary Benefit, CEAC curves.

## Pillar 2: Executable Analytics & Computable R Markdown Pipeline

### 2.1 Pipeline Architecture

The project implements a literate programming architecture where analytical workflows are authored as computable R Markdown documents in `StandardStudies/rmd/*.Rmd` and knitted directly into Jekyll includes in `_includes/rmd_output/*.md`.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["StandardStudies/rmd/*.Rmd<br>(Computable Source)"] -->|rmarkdown::render| B["_includes/rmd_output/*.md<br>(GitHub Markdown)"]
    A -->|knitr output| C["assets/images/rmd_output/*.png<br>(Rendered Plots)"]
    B -->|{% include %}| D["docs/data_analysis/standard_studies/*.md<br>(Jekyll Pages)"]
    C -->|Static Image URL| D
```

#### Critical Finding: DOM Bloat & Build Degradation
* **Issue**: `_includes/rmd_output/comparative_cohort.md` contains **7.0 MB** and `population_level_epidemiology.md` contains **1.2 MB** of raw uncompressed HTML/CSS generated by `gt` tables.
* **Impact**:
  * Hugely inflates Jekyll HTML page generation time.
  * Causes massive DOM size in user browsers (>20,000 HTML elements), degrading page scroll and render performance on mobile/low-memory devices.
* **Remediation**:
  * In Rmd source files, configure `visOmopResults` / `gt` outputs to use markdown table formatting (`format = "markdown"` or `as_raw_html = FALSE`), or limit printed table preview rows (`head(n = 10)` or `options(gt.html_table_head = TRUE)`), or render summary tables as compact markdown tibbles.

### 2.3 Asset Routing & Orphaned Files Audit

  * *Action*: Remove this orphaned subfolder to maintain a clean repository tree.

### 3.3 Catalog Discrepancies & Naming Inconsistencies

1. **Catalog Synchronization Discrepancies**:
   * `OmopStudyBuilder` is documented in `package_reference/OmopStudyBuilder.md` and included in the `index.md` architecture diagram, but is **missing from `README.md`** and **missing from `utils/libraries.csv`**.
   * `PatientLevelPrediction` is discussed in `package_reference/index.md` and `standard_studies/patient_level_prediction.md`, but links to external OHDSI documentation rather than having a local package reference page. (This is appropriate given it belongs to HADES rather than DARWIN EU, but should be consistently noted).
2. **Package Filename Casing Inconsistencies**:
   * Some files use all-lowercase names while others use PascalCase:
     * Lowercase: `cdmconnector.md`, `codelistgenerator.md`, `cohortcharacteristics.md`, `cohortconstructor.md`, `cohortsurvival.md`, `drugutilisation.md`, `omopgenerics.md`, `omopsketch.md`, `phenotyper.md`, `visomopresults.md`.
     * PascalCase: `CohortCosts.md`, `CohortEconomics.md`, `CohortSymmetry.md`, `CohortUtilisation.md`, `EpiStandard.md`, `IncidencePrevalence.md`, `MeasurementDiagnostics.md`, `OmopConstructor.md`, `OmopStudyBuilder.md`, `OmopViewer.md`, `PatientProfiles.md`.
   * *Recommendation*: Standardize to the exact official R package capitalization (e.g., `CDMConnector.md`, `CohortConstructor.md`, `PhenotypeR.md`, `visOmopResults.md`) with clean Jekyll URL aliases or case-insensitive permalinks.
3. **Installation Command Standardization**:
   * Various package docs use `devtools::install_github()`, `remotes::install_github()`, or `pak::pkg_install()`.
   * Modern R best practice across the DARWIN EU and OHDSI community standardizes on `pak::pkg_install()`.
